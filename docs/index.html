Teach a car to race round tracks it has never seen

The agent drives Gymnasium's CarRacing-v3 from 49 numbers (speed, grip, where the road goes, and 19 lidar rays) instead of pixels. The goal: a mean score of 900 (the classic "solved" level) on 100 random tracks it never trained on, driving deterministically under CarRacing's own rules: +1000/N for each of the track's N tiles, −0.1 per frame, 1000 frames. Method: an actor-critic (A2C, now trained with its sibling PPO) learning from millions of simulated laps. The first version scored −43: its lidar was blind.
